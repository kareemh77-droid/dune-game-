Spellsiege

Two castles, two warbands. Each side brings a Giant, a Wizard, a mounted Knight and a Goblin bomber. Last band standing wins.

How to play: each turn you control one fighter. Pick one of their four attacks (tap it to see what it does), drag across the battlefield to aim, then tap Attack. A longer drag means a stronger throw. Pinch to zoom; the magnifier shows the whole map. Anyone who falls in water is out.
