Spellsiege

Two castles, two warbands. Each side brings a Giant, a Wizard, a mounted Knight, a Goblin bomber, an Undead Warrior and a Dragon. Last band standing wins.

How to play: each turn you control one fighter. Pick one of their four attacks (tap it to see what it does), drag across the battlefield to aim, then tap Attack. A longer drag means a stronger throw. Pinch to zoom; the magnifier shows the whole map. Castles are open: walk through the gates, climb the stairs, jump up through wooden floors, and press Jump at a ladder to climb it. The Undead Warrior's Soul bar fills whenever anyone gets hurt; when it's full he can raise the Army of the Dead. The Dragon hovers, so it glides over gaps and ignores toxic clouds and lava. Anyone who falls in water is out.
