Spellsiege

Two circles of wizards, two castles, one very breakable kingdom. Last circle standing wins.

How to play: drag across the battlefield to aim. A longer drag means a stronger spell. Then tap Cast. Pinch to zoom, and tap the magnifier to see the whole map. Wind pushes fireballs and meteors, but not runes. Runes bounce and go off after 3 seconds. Anyone who falls in the moat is out.
